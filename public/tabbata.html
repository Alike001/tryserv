    Tabbas reviews the documents supplied in this workspace only. It does not verify physical delivery, receipt authenticity, expenditure eligibility, fraud, audit compliance or funding approval. A human reviewer remains responsible for every decision.

    WHAT TABBAS DOES NOT CLAIM

    Documents are evidence.
    Not the whole truth.

    Tabbas reviews the contents of the files you provide. It cannot confirm that a facility exists, that a person attended an activity, that a receipt is genuine or that money was spent appropriately.

    A missing source means it was not found in this bundle—not that the claim is false. A supported passage means the submitted documents support the wording—not that the real-world event has been independently verified.

    Tabbas is not an auditor, accountant, funder or legal adviser. A human reviewer remains responsible for all decisions.

    Original uploaded files are not retained. Extracted text, structured CSV/XLSX cell values, source locators and review outputs are stored in a private case for up to 24 hours; you can delete the case sooner.
