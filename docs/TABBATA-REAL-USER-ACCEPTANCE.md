# Tabbas — Real-User Acceptance Plan

**Status:** Not yet conducted. This document is a test plan, not evidence that human testing happened.

## Participants to recruit

1. One Nigerian NGO programme/M&E officer who has prepared a donor narrative report.
2. One finance officer or report reviewer who has reconciled donor expenditure schedules.

Do not ask participants to upload real beneficiary data or confidential donor records. Use the fictional sample pack unless the organisation provides an explicitly authorised, redacted test bundle.

## Session rules

- Ask participants to share their screen and think aloud.
- Do not coach them through the task unless they are blocked.
- Record time to complete, misunderstandings, errors and exact feedback.
- Ask permission before recording audio/video.
- Do not collect names of beneficiaries, bank details, national IDs, health data or confidential donor information.
- Do not claim a participant approved the product; report their actual words and limitations.

## Task A — Programme/M&E officer

"Please review this fictional quarterly report pack before it is sent to a funder. Identify anything that needs follow-up and show where the supporting information came from."

Observe:
- Can they understand the product in under 30 seconds?
- Can they enter funder requirements without help?
- Do they understand the difference between narrative report and supporting evidence?
- Can they identify which finding needs clarification?
- Do they trust and understand the source locator?
- Do they understand that a missing document is not proof that the activity did not happen?

## Task B — Finance officer / reviewer

"Please compare the entered approved budget and reported expenditure, inspect the financial schedule and explain what you would check next."

Observe:
- Do they understand that the budget result is arithmetic on entered totals?
- Can they find the relevant financial row?
- Do they expect line-item reconciliation that is not yet implemented?
- Can they distinguish a budget overrun from an ineligible expense?
- Do they understand that Tabbas is not an audit or funder approval?

## Interview questions

1. How do you currently prepare and check this type of report?
2. Which part takes the most time?
3. What errors or omissions most often cause a report to be returned?
4. Which documents do you usually compare?
5. How do you trace a narrative claim back to a supporting record?
6. Would this workflow fit before your current donor submission process? Why or why not?
7. What would stop you from uploading documents to this service?
8. What must be improved before you would use it with a real report?
9. Would you prefer a per-report fee or a team subscription? What price range would be reasonable?
10. Who would approve payment for a tool like this?

## Results template

For each participant record:
- Role (not personal name unless they consent).
- Relevant reporting experience.
- Task completed / not completed.
- Time taken.
- Steps completed without assistance.
- Confusions and blockers.
- Direct feedback quotes (with permission).
- Feature requests.
- Privacy concerns.
- Willingness-to-pay signal (not a purchase commitment).
- Follow-up changes.

## Acceptance threshold

Do not claim validation until both roles have completed the task. A useful initial signal is that each participant can create a review, inspect at least one source-linked finding, correctly explain the limitations, and identify a concrete follow-up without coaching.
